Stash privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, email and profile picture URL (scopes: openid, email, profile). We never receive your Google password.
  • Your content: the expenses, credits, friends, links, notes, todos and friend photos you add. They are saved on your phone and synced to our database so you can use them on your other devices.

How it is used

  • Only to run the app for you: sign-in, saving and syncing your data.
  • Each account can only read its own data (row-level security). Friend photos are stored in private storage that only your account can access.
  • If you turn on "Add todos to calendar", Stash writes your dated todos to the calendar you choose on your phone. Stash does not read your other events.
  • To show link previews, the app downloads the public web page of links you save.
  • Notifications for reminders are scheduled locally on your phone.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data with third parties.

Where data is stored

Data is hosted on Supabase (database and storage). Google sign-in is provided by Google.

Deleting your data

Email us to delete your account and all its data. Signing out also removes your data from that phone.
